The PC of the future.
In your pocket.

COMPACT. FLEXIBLE. TOGETHER. ANYWHERE.

From coding to running apps, all on this one device. No additional peripherals required.

AREA512 is a portable development terminal for people who work away from a desk. Write it on the train, run it at the desk — the terminal does not care which. Compact, reliable, and ready for your ideas, wherever you are.

  • File management
  • Development tools
  • Built-in applications
  • Customizable interface

Built-in applications for business use.

WriterText editing. Japanese and English input. Ctrl+Space switches language.Calc26 × 26 cells. Arithmetic, cell references, and range functions.SchedulerMonthly calendar with per-day events. Saving is manual.

Also included: Paint, Solitair, Bomb, Space Lander, Slide, Gallery, Theme.
