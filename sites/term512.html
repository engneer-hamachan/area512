TERM512

Dedicated hardware for AREA512. External display, folding enclosure, front latches, and built-in stand.

Display and enclosure

TERM512 is dedicated hardware for AREA512. It combines an external display with a folding enclosure.

The enclosure has two front latches and a built-in folding stand.

FEATURES

DISPLAY
Integrated external screen
ENCLOSURE
Folding case with front locks
SUPPORT
Built-in folding stand

Front latches

Two front latches hold the enclosure closed.

Folding stand

The built-in stand supports the terminal on a desk.

Rear storage position

The stand folds against the back of the enclosure.
